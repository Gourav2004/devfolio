How I work

Engineering standards

The habits that carry over from project to project, regardless of the stack.

01

Ship what's maintainable

Clean structure over clever code, so the person who touches it next — often me, months later — isn't fighting the codebase.

02

Own the full stack

Frontend, backend, and deployment are one job, not three handoffs. Fewer seams means fewer things break silently.

03

Design for the real user

Interfaces are built for the person who has to use them daily — usually not a technical one — not for a portfolio screenshot.

04

Scope honestly

I'd rather tell you what a timeline or budget actually allows than overpromise and cut corners quietly later.
